XAHBall is HaxBall-style football for the classic Amiga: one screen, a round-cornered pitch, discs for players, a ball that pings off the posts – and up to six people crowded around one A500.

Move, hold fire, kick. One kick per press, the ball keeps its momentum, and a post can save you or ruin you.

Features

  • 1 vs 1, 2 vs 2 and 3 vs 3 – everyone on one Amiga
  • Ready-made controller setups: joystick in port 2 vs CPU, two joysticks, joystick vs keyboard, up to four joysticks (with a parallel-port 4-player adapter) plus two players on the keyboard – or set every player up yourself
  • CPU players fill every empty slot, NORMAL or HARD – the hard ones read where the ball is going and put a keeper in goal
  • 12 national teams with flags and kit colors, away kits when colors clash
  • Match time 1–5 minutes; the clock only runs while the ball is in play
  • Optional rules: kickoff circle lock, ball freeze on goals
  • Demo mode – leave it alone and the computer plays a match
  • 50 fps on a stock Amiga 500

Controls

  • Joystick: move, fire to kick (hold to charge, one kick per press)
  • Keyboard player 1: arrows + RIGHT SHIFT
  • Keyboard player 2: W S A D + LEFT SHIFT
  • Menus: joystick in port 2, or arrows/WSAD + RETURN; ESC goes back. ESC pauses a match, ESC again quits.

Requirements

  • Amiga 500/500+/600/1200 or an emulator (FS-UAE, WinUAE, Amiberry)
  • 68000, 1 MB RAM (512K chip + 512K slow or fast), Kickstart 1.3 or newer, PAL
  • Hard drive or a folder (no floppy image yet)
  • Optional: parallel-port 4-player adapter for joysticks in ports 3 and 4

Install

Unpack xahball.lha anywhere on your hard drive, open the xahball drawer and run xahball.exe from a shell inside that drawer (it loads its data from data/ next to it). If the executable lost its “e” flag on the way: protect xahball.exe e add.
